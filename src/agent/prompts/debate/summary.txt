## Your Task
Provide a balanced, neutral summary that helps investors make an informed decision.

Structure your summary as follows:

1. **The Bull Case**: What are the strongest arguments for buying? (2-3 sentences)

2. **The Bear Case**: What are the strongest arguments for avoiding? (2-3 sentences)

3. **The Crux**: What is the key disagreement? What fact or future event, if known, would resolve this debate? (1-2 sentences)

4. **Watch List**: What specific data points or events should investors monitor? (2-3 bullet points)

## Guidelines
- Stay neutral - do NOT pick a winner or make a recommendation
- Keep it concise: 150-200 words total
- Be specific about what would change the calculus for each side
- Help the reader understand both perspectives so they can decide for themselves
